CAGE Distance Analyzer

Ghemawat's four distances (Cultural, Administrative, Geographic, Economic) between two countries, weighted for an industry. Every score is built from cited country-level data. Change the inputs below and the page recalculates.

1 · The four dimensions

Each dimension is scored 0 (no distance) to 10 (extreme distance) from weighted sub-factors. Continuous measures are scored by percentile rank among all 2,070 ordered country pairs in the tool, so 8.0 means more distant than 80% of pairs. Categorical measures use the fixed rules shown in the Method section.
